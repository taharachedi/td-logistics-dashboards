My addition: which warehouse is least CO2-efficient per tonne? (bar of kg CO2/tonne)

The page's own decision statement asks which warehouse is the least CO2-efficient per tonne delivered, but neither chart above answers it: the ranking shows routes, not warehouses, and the map shows totals, which mix "far" with "big". This chart divides CO2 by tonnes delivered, so a warehouse serving many tonnes is not penalised just for its size. A bar chart (not a pie) because the values are rates, not shares of a whole — a pie of kg/tonne would mean nothing. The dashed line is the network average, so "worse than average" reads at a glance.
